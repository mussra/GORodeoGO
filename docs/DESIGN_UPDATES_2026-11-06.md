# Design updates - 6 November 2026

## Rare horses and powers (07:59)
Ant explicitly revised the earlier separation between rarity and power: rarer horses should be able to have special abilities or superpowers. Running faster and a super dodge were examples, not final values or rules. Mechanics, balance, unlocks, drawbacks and inheritance remain undecided. Preserve earlier notes as history; do not treat cosmetic-only rarity as a current restriction. No powers are implemented by this note.

## Intentional head inheritance (07:55)
Ant wants to explore inheriting the body coat from one parent and either the complete head or only the forehead-to-muzzle stripe from the other. This is a new intentional trait choice, separate from the accidental brown face in the foal mask. Preview the choices before fixing final rules. No arbitrary dominance, rates, prices or powers follow from this idea.

## Mask repairs and rainbow (07:55-07:56)
The foal's fixed brown face and rainbow socks are mask errors, not inherited traits. Mane/tail color must not recolor legs. Keep the source illustration, original version and masks in history. Rainbow transitions on the small foal should be smoother, retaining recognizable colors. No rainbow-leg gene is approved.

## HD portraits (07:51)
The hidden-face adult concept is approved for large views, menu and shop comparison. It must reflect the selected horse, hat and actual equipment. It does not approve replacing small gameplay sprites, gallop animation, imaginary equipment or a fixed common illustration masquerading as selection.

## Fence jumping power candidate (08:03)
Ant proposes particular horses being able to jump certain fences while bulls must go around. Candidate uses include leaving a pen and shortcuts. This is not a confirmed map bug and does not approve changing current maps. Decide what happens to lassoed bulls before implementing. Controls, cost, cooldown, rarity and requirements remain open.

## Adult training before breeding candidate (08:04)
Ant proposes using and training each adult through completed missions before it can breed. The goal is to link collection, appearance, powers and play rather than leave breeding parents unused. Counts and requirements remain open. This is distinct from foal growth and initial training. No new gates are added to the isolated trial. Preferring completed missions over entering/exiting games, and avoiding forced repetition, are recommendations, not approved final rules.
